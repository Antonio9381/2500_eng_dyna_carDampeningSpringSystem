\section{Conclusions}

This report analysed a simplified model of a car's suspension systems to try and find optimal spring and damping coefficients. The system was solved numerically, and the results plotted.

The results showed that an optimal solution did exist, and it is when $c=1500$ Ns/m and $k=34.5$ kN/m.

These findings have important implications for providing a smooth driving experience and is important to model since its more efficient and cost-effective than random trial and error testing.

Future work could explore making a more complex 3-D simulation to analyse displacement, pitch and roll, along with trying different road simulations.
